# Harbour Baths
domain: architecture
audience: graduate admissions
keywords: harbour, timber, public bath, threshold

## Concept
A public bath sits on the edge of the old harbour, where a timber deck folds into a sequence of pools and a long roof shelters the walk between them. The building is a threshold between the town and the water: the pools step down with the tide, and the changing rooms are tucked under the deck so the horizon stays open.

## Site
The site is a disused quay with a stone edge and a working slipway. The deck keeps the quay line and lifts only where the baths need depth.

## Structure
Glulam portal frames at three-metre centres carry the roof; the deck is a sacrificial larch skin over a steel grillage.

## Role
Individual work, 12 weeks, third year.
