**Top 3 issues**

2. page 4, severity medium, category whitespace_intent: "Text blocks float at three unrelated heights beside the plan; ~30% of the page is empty at the bottom." Evidence: p4 title, one-liner and caption. Route: `local_refine`.
3. page 8, severity medium, category asset_fidelity: "Index thumbnails are ~100 px and the sheets are illegible. The 09 thumbnail overruns the right edge, and the left text column is scattered." Evidence: p8 grid. Route: `pivot_layout`.

## Ranking

**1. Z, 2. Y, 3. X.** Y beats X because it adds the analysis page and a windows page, and it shows the elevations and fireplace large. X is the most template-like and has the worst thumbnail pages.

Z does what neither X nor Y does. It gives the book a single visual identity (cream paper, brick accent, one type system, eyebrow labels, running footer). It also keeps every survey sheet large and aligned to a page rhythm that varies from cover to hero to plates to index, without filler diagrams.

X and Y share the generic white template and a thumbnail page that duplicates later pages. Y's analysis page is the one thing Z lacks.
