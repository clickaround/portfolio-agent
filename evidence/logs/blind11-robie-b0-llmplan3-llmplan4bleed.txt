**Top 3 issues**

3. `page 4`, severity **medium**, `whitespace_intent`: The index thumbnails are illegible and the layout is lopsided. Evidence: a 6-up row of thumbnails with a large blank bottom. Route: `pivot_layout`.

## Ranking

**1. Y (0.72), 2. Z (0.59), 3. X (0.55).** All three need a revision, and none passes. Z and X are close: Z's p8 is strong, but its p10 crop and p11 placeholder pull it down. X's p3, p5 and p7 are good, but its p8 and p9 are worse.

**What the best one does that the others do not:** Y is the only book with a designed cover, a consistent warm palette and header/footer system, and a different layout on nearly every page. Its drawings stay large and unclipped, and it leaves out the placeholder diagram pages that hurt X and Z.
