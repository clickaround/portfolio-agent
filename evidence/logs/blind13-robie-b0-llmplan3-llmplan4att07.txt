## Ranking

**Z > Y > X.** None passes.

Z is the only book that holds a consistent grid and system across every page. Each page has an eyebrow, a rule, a serif headline, a body line and a caption, and the drawings stay large and crisp throughout. It also shows the plans in full instead of as thumbnails, and it has no placeholder content.

Y has the best editorial moments: the full-bleed cover and the p3 card with a headline. Its detail pages are thumbnail-sized, and the colour and type system is less consistent. X's 12 pages are padded with weak pages (p4 and p11 are the worst), and it has the cropped sheet on p10.
