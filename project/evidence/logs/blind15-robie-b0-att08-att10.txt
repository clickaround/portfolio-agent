## Ranking

**Y > X > Z.** Y is the only candidate near the pass line. X is the same content as Z with a stronger frame, and it drops Z's weak index page, so X beats Z.

**What the best one (Y) does that the others do not:** Y has a real cover, with a hero photo and a title block in a clear hierarchy. A consistent eyebrow-rule-title system and the same footer on every page give it a recognizable editorial voice. Its section/elevation and window pages place the drawings large and legible with short captions, and it avoids the placeholder diagram page and the Korean legend text that weaken X and Z.

None of the three passes. Y's gap to pass is mainly the p8 index and the unbalanced pages (p2, p4, p6). X would need the p10 diagrams and the p4 legend fixed.
