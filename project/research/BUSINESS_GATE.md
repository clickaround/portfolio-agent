# Business evidence gate

Hypotheses to falsify: users pay for high-quality portfolio, local edit saves time, print fidelity is valued, repeated application customization matters, studios have recurrent demand.

Interview/sample-plan: actual output and baseline shown blind at same print size, ask user to complete task, record their edit time and corrections; offer an explicit paid pilot, record actual completed payment (not just verbal interest), churn/one-time purchase intent. Collect direct model/compute cost, customer support minutes, refund risk, permissions overhead. No universal monthly subscription assumptions; private project data storage must be clear. Do not claim market validation from public complaints or social-media engagement.
